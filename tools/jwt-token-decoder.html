Technical Notes

          
Splits the token on '.' delimiters and Base64Url-decodes the Header (Part 1) and Payload Claims (Part 2), formatting `iat` and `exp` Unix timestamps into ISO dates.

          
Frequently Asked Questions

          
Why is it safer to decode JWTs in a client-side static tool?
Many online decoders log requests on their servers. Because this page is a static client-side script, your token never leaves your browser tab.
