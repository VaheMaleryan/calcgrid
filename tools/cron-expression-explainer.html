Technical Notes

          
Standard 5-field Cron syntax: [Minute 0-59] [Hour 0-23] [Day of Month 1-31] [Month 1-12] [Day of Week 0-6 (Sun-Sat)].

          
Frequently Asked Questions

          
What does */15 mean in a cron expression?
The slash (/) specifies step values—*/15 in the minute field runs the job every 15 minutes (at :00, :15, :30, and :45).
How do I run a cron job only on weekdays?
Set the 5th field (Day of Week) to 1-5 (Monday through Friday).
