Reference Notes & FAQ

What is the difference between Advertiser eCPM and Publisher Page RPM?

eCPM is the gross cost per 1,000 ad impressions before the ad network or portal takes its cut. Google AdSense typically gives publishers 68% of content ad revenue.

Why do finance and developer utility pages have higher eCPMs than gaming?

B2B software and financial advertisers pay much higher CPC bids than casual entertainment advertisers, resulting in higher RPMs per visitor.
